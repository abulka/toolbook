Note to Toolbook Developers

If you are running the toolbook 6 or toolbook 4 development environment and are fearful of installing a toolbook 5 runtime on your system - don't worry.  The special Toolbook 5 install routine included in this package does not alter your .TBK or TBLOAD associations and is a totally self-contained installation.
